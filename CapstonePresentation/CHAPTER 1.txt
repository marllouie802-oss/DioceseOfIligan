CHAPTER 1

Introduction
In today’s digital age, technology has revolutionized the way people give, connect, and stay informed. Parish and charitable organizations are increasingly adopting online platforms to make donations, event management, and communication easier and more accessible for everyone. These digital systems serve as important bridges between donors, organizations, and the communities they aim to help, allowing for faster transactions, greater transparency, and more effective engagement.
One of the activities that is currently following the digital trend is online donation. Now, many non-profit organizations are starting to leave conventional ways and switch to using digital platforms to raise donations. This is also supported by the rapid growth of financial technology (fintech) companies that have created digital wallets and make it easier for people to donate through their phones. This phenomenon led to the emergence of online fundraising platforms, commonly known as donation-based crowdfunding, where the nominal of donation is decreased but the amount is increased. The increase in digital donation through crowdfunding platform in Indonesia has more than doubled since 2017 [1].
One of the reasons driving the rise of parish online giving platforms is convenience. Gone are the times when individuals carried cash or wrote checks for donations as a practice. Now, with a few taps on their smartphones or computers, parish members can promptly support their congregation financially whenever they feel inclined to do so. The accessibility and simplicity provided by these tools ensure that everyone has an opportunity to contribute to their parish without being hindered by challenges.
The future of parish donations lies in utilizing the giving platforms. Their easy usability, user design, security features, and wider reach make them essential tools for parish looking to incorporate digital solutions while meeting the changing needs and expectations of their members. By integrating these platforms into their systems, parish can boost community involvement, improve transparency, and establish a strong base to sustain their missions in the long term [2].
A truly comprehensive event management system will allow users and organizers to access and manage all aspects of an event, including registration, marketing, engagement, integrations, physical planning and preparation, reporting and analytics, and more.  
On college and university campuses, events are happening all the time, whether student- or faculty-focused, and often through in-house or third parties and vendors. When these events take place, it is critical to use an event management system that offers full control and planning capabilities to ensure activities happen on time, in accommodating spaces and keeping in mind the safety of every attending. Comprehensive event planning software will allow campus event planners to book large-scale events, communicate with service providers, and maintain a single source of record for repeatable, measurable events [3].
The researchers developed an integrated digital platform for the Diocese of Iligan to efficiently manage parish administration and enhance community engagement across Roman Catholic parishes in Iligan City, Philippines. This platform streamlines monetary and goods donations through secure payment integration (PayPal and credit card), sacramental service bookings (baptism, weddings, confessions, masses), and the distribution of digital bulletins and sermon summaries.
Parish administrators can manage donation campaigns, coordinate service bookings with intelligent scheduling, post announcements, and deliver updates to the congregation. Citizens can discover and follow their parish parishes, book services, make donations, read bulletins, and engage with parish content through social features including likes, comments, and bookmarks.

Statement of the Problem
The researchers have recognized the following problems that highlight the need for a digital solution to improve parish operations and communication. These issues reflect the limitations of current manual systems used by parish in Iligan City, which hinder efficient management of donations, events, and information sharing:
1. Despite the active participation of citizen in outreach and religious activities, many parishes in Iligan City lack a centralized and accessible platform for managing donations, making it challenging to coordinate, track, and distribute goods for parish-led outreach programs efficiently.
2. Unlike businesses and organizations that have their own digital platforms to promote services and engage customers, most parishes in Iligan City do not have a dedicated system to post bulletins, announcements, sermon summaries, or spiritual content. Instead, they rely on third-party social media platforms (Facebook, messaging apps) that are fragmented, unreliable, and not designed for parish-specific needs. This dependency results in inconsistent communication, limited reach to citizens without social media accounts (particularly senior citizens), and inability to centralize parish information in one accessible location. A dedicated platform integrating bulletins, announcements, and service bookings would allow citizens to receive updates directly while interacting with the system, making communication more efficient, centralized, and accessible for all community members.
3. The use of outdated and manual methods for handling sacramental service bookings (baptisms, weddings, confessions, masses) and event reservations in parishes across Iligan City leads to significant inefficiencies in organizing parish activities. Traditional systems relying on phone calls, walk-in appointments, and paper-based scheduling often result in double bookings, scheduling conflicts, miscommunication between parish staff and citizens, delayed confirmations, and poor allocation of parish resources such as clergy time and parish facilities. These inefficiencies affect overall parish operations, reduce member participation due to booking difficulties, and create administrative burden for parish staff who must manually coordinate schedules and send reminders.

Objectives of the Study
The objective of this study is to design and develop an integrated digital platform specifically for the parishes of the Diocese in Iligan City, Philippines. The following are the specific objectives:
1. To design and develop a centralized donation management system that enables parishes in Iligan City to efficiently accept, process, track, and manage monetary and goods donations through secure payment integration (PayPal and credit card), with features for fundraising campaign creation, goal tracking, progress visualization, and transparent reporting to support parish-led outreach programs and charitable activities.
2. To develop a built-in content posting and bulletin distribution feature within the platform that allows parishes to directly share digital bulletins, announcements, sermon summaries, and spiritual content with citizens, providing a centralized, accessible alternative to third-party social media platforms and enabling community engagement through social features such as likes, comments, and bookmarks.
3. To incorporate a sacramental service booking and event coordination system into the digital platform, enabling parish administrators and staff to efficiently schedule, organize, and manage sacramental services (baptisms, weddings, confessions, masses) and parish activities through intelligent scheduling with conflict detection, automated notifications, and optimized allocation of parish resources including clergy time and parish facilities.

Scope and Limitations
	This study was conducted in Iligan City, Philippines, focusing on the development and evaluation of an integrated digital platform for the Diocese of Iligan. The main purpose of this study is to develop a web-based system that allows parish administrators, citizens and citizens to manage monetary and goods donations, book sacramental services, coordinate events, and receive digital bulletins, announcements, and sermon summaries. 
The platform is designed to improve current manual methods and ensure that parish leaders and community members stay well-informed and connected, making it easier to organize outreach activities and parish events The platform provides comprehensive features for both parish administrators and citizens. Parish administrators, including parish owners, managers, and staff, can create and manage parish profiles with comprehensive information such as address, contact details, mass schedules, and ministries. They can accept and process donations through secure payment integration with PayPal and credit card, create fundraising campaigns with goal tracking and progress visualization, and manage sacramental service bookings including baptisms, weddings, confessions, and masses with intelligent scheduling and conflict detection. Administrators can also post digital bulletins, announcements, sermon summaries, and spiritual content, coordinate parish events and activities, review and approve or decline booking requests, access analytics dashboards showing donation trends and booking patterns, and generate reports for transparency and decision-making.
For citizens, the system allows them to discover and follow parishes in Iligan City, make monetary donations through secure payment processing, and book sacramental services by selecting service type, date, and time. Citizens can view their appointment status including pending, approved, or completed bookings along with booking invoices, read parish bulletins and announcements on their feed, and engage with parish content through social features such as liking, commenting, and bookmarking posts. They also receive real-time notifications about booking status, upcoming appointments, and new parish posts, and can access the platform through web browsers on desktop and mobile devices.
The system is built using Django 5.2.6 framework with PostgreSQL database for robust data management and cloud-based storage for media files such as parish logos, cover images, and service photos. The platform implements role-based access controls ensuring appropriate delegation of administrative responsibilities while maintaining security through email verification, IP tracking, and rate limiting. Philippine-specific features, including localized address structures with region, province, city or municipality, and barangay fields, ensure the system meets the unique needs of Filipino parishes.
All records of donations, service bookings, event participation, and community interactions are securely stored in the database for easy viewing and management. The system generates comprehensive reports based on entered data, helping parish staff track donation progress, analyze booking patterns, monitor community engagement, and make data-driven decisions to improve parish operations over time.
Despite the system's comprehensive features, several limitations were identified during development and evaluation. The system requires a stable internet connection to function, meaning parishes or parishioners in areas with unreliable internet connectivity may experience difficulties accessing the platform or completing transactions, as offline functionality is not included in the current implementation. The current system supports only PayPal and credit card payments, and the usability evaluation revealed this as a critical barrier with a score of 3.1 out of 5, particularly for ordinary citizens unfamiliar with international payment platforms. Local Filipino payment methods such as GCash, PayMaya, and bank transfers are not yet integrated.
The evaluation revealed significant usability challenges for ordinary citizens, who achieved a SUS score of 66.8, particularly affecting senior citizens and those with limited technology experience. Issues include inadequate text size scoring 3.4 out of 5, small button targets scoring 3.5 out of 5, a confusing booking calendar interface scoring 3.2 out of 5, and complex booking forms scoring 3.3 out of 5. These accessibility barriers limit independent system use for a significant portion of the parish community. Additionally, the system is currently available only in English, and multi-language support for Filipino, Cebuano, and other regional languages spoken in Iligan City is not included, potentially limiting accessibility for citizens more comfortable with local languages.
Several advanced features are not included in the current scope, including live streaming of masses and parish events, mobile native applications for iOS and Android, integration with external calendar systems such as Google Calendar or Outlook, advanced data analytics and predictive insights, automated SMS notifications as only email and in-app notifications are supported, community forum or discussion board features, and integration with other parish management systems or diocesan networks. The system does not include provisions for ongoing technical support, user training programs, or long-term maintenance after deployment, and parish administrators are expected to manage the system independently, though the evaluation revealed that some users may require technical assistance with a score of 2.6 out of 5 for needing help.
This study focuses specifically on parishes within the Diocese of Iligan in Iligan City, Philippines, and the system is not designed for inter-diocesan coordination or promoting activities outside the local parish community. Integration with broader parish networks or national parish management systems is beyond the scope of this research. While the system implements basic security measures including email verification, IP tracking, rate limiting, and role-based access controls, advanced data protection features such as two-factor authentication, end-to-end encryption for sensitive data, and comprehensive security audits are not included in the current implementation.
