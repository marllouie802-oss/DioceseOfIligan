Summary of Findings

Finding 1: Critical User Experience Gap

Parish staff rated the platform 85.0 while 48 citizens scored it 57.9 — a 27-point divide.

  • Staff scored ease of use and confidence at 100 points each
  • Half of citizens found the platform cumbersome and required significant learning
  • System designed for administrators, not the community it serves

Finding 2: Strong Technical Foundation, Poor User Experience

Citizens valued consistency, feature integration, and willingness to use regularly (68.25).

  • Technical foundation is sound — features work well together
  • Critical failure points: donation and booking processes
  • Lowest scores: confidence, learning curve, cumbersome navigation
  • People want to use the platform but feel inadequate when attempting

Finding 3: Assumption of Universal Comfort Level

The 27-point difference reveals system assumes everyone has administrator-level comfort.

  • Staff rated learnability at 75.00
  • Citizens rated it at 50.00
  • Citizens need time and technical support just to get started
  • Platform must meet people at their level, not administrator expectations

Key Issues Identified:

  • Confidence Gap: Only 50.80% of users feel confident navigating independently
  • Technical Support Dependency: 48.83 (lowest score) — most think they need technical assistance
  • Steep Learning Curve: 46.26 score for ease of learning
  • Cumbersome Experience: 46.26 — system feels difficult to use
  • Barrier for Seniors: Elderly parishioners avoid the system instead of seeking help

The Good News

The basic features are solid — they just need better scaffolding. The 58.6 score is not a verdict, it's a roadmap. Close the confidence gap, ease the learning process, and eliminate technical support dependency.

Recommendations

Critical fixes needed to make the Diocese of Iligan platform work for everyone, not just administrators:

1. Fix Payment System Critical

  • Add GCash and PayMaya — what Filipinos actually use
  • Add bank transfer with clear instructions
  • Simplify payment flow — no confusing redirects
  • Show security badges for user trust

2. Improve User Confidence Critical

  • Interactive onboarding guidelines
  • Enhanced navigation with breadcrumbs
  • Reduce form fields to essentials only
  • Add progress indicators throughout

3. Build User Support High Priority

  • More confirmation messages
  • Undo buttons for user mistakes
  • Helpful error messages
  • 5-step tutorial for first-time users

4. Senior-Friendly Design High Priority

  • Consider a "Senior Mode" with larger text
  • Fewer options to reduce cognitive load
  • Larger buttons and touch targets
  • Simple, clear language throughout

5. Enhanced Learning Support Moderate

  • Video tutorials in Filipino and English
  • Tooltips for unfamiliar terms
  • "What's this?" context buttons
  • Phone support during business hours

6. Continuous Improvement Future

  • Test with real parishioners of varying ages
  • Observe where users struggle
  • Listen to user frustrations
  • Iterate based on actual usage patterns

Goal: Transform the platform from "functional competence" to "actual accessibility for all"
