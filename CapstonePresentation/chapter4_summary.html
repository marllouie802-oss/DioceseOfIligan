Interpretation

  • The platform works but doesn't work well for all users – there's a critical 30-point gap between staff (87.5) and citizens (57.9).
  • System is designed for administrators, not parishioners: Staff use it daily and know workarounds, but 96% of users (citizens) struggle.
  • Technical capability vs. usability gap: The system can do what it needs to do, but makes people work too hard to do it.
  • Barrier to parish engagement: Complex processes prevent meaningful interaction with parish community.
  • Need for user-centered design: System assumes everyone knows what they're doing, causing half the community to struggle.
